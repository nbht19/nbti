# PNR

## Traits

- 探求型: 教えや体験の意味を深く考え、真理を求める姿勢
- 逆境からの転換型: 困難や試練を越えて生まれ変わる感覚
- 受容型: 外に語るよりも、内側で深く受けとめる静かな力

## Description

スモーキーモーヴ背景に、不規則な多角形の層・小さな中心点・内向きの切り込みを使った抽象アイコンです。困難の奥で真理を探り、内側に深く沈めていく印象を表します。

## Prompt

```txt

Use this unique color palette for PNR: soft smoky mauve background (#E9DDE5), near-black violet layers (#2F293E), muted blue-gray facets (#65738A), old gold center point (#B8974A), and charcoal details (#292B32). Do not use white, off-white, transparent, or blank backgrounds. Use the specified background color as a full-canvas solid fill. Use flat color with only very subtle gradients if necessary.

The icon should feel like part of a consistent app icon system: thin 1.5px-style linework, balanced spacing, simple geometry, large empty margins, and enough clarity to work at small mobile sizes. Prefer outline shapes over filled shapes.

Type PNR icon: construct a compact geometric mark made of nested irregular thin polygon outlines, a small central point, and inward-facing triangular notch lines. Do not depict a cave, crystal, star, landscape, or object.

Trait translation: inquiry, adversity-to-renewal, inward receptivity. Represent these only through geometry: nested polygons, broken outer edge, small central point, inward notches.

Composition: quiet, exact, inward-focused, not dense. Use near-black violet thin polygon outlines, muted blue-gray line facets, and one old gold center point on the soft smoky mauve background.

Negative prompt: no thick strokes, no fat lines, no heavy filled shapes, no bulky icon, no dense ornament, no complex illustration, no soft blobs, no bubbly shapes, No text, no letters, no typography, no people, no faces, no animals, no plants, no cave, no crystal, no starry sky, no landscape, no glowing fantasy scene, no mascot, no anime, no illustration scene, no hand-drawn style, no 3D render, no photorealism, no religious symbols, no cross, no temple, no clutter, no messy lines, no busy details, no white background, no off-white background, no transparent background, no blank background.
```
